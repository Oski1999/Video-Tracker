## Video 7: Bible Teaching

**Run time:** about 2:10 · **For:** Junior and Senior High teachers

*(Cold open: a student teacher holding up a visual aid to a row of younger kids.)*

**[P1]** Picture a room full of fourth to sixth graders. You've got a few minutes to teach them something from the Bible they'll remember. That's Bible Teaching.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for teachers.

### Who and how long

*(Graphic: time limits by division.)*

**[P1]** Bible Teaching is open to young men and young women in Junior and Senior High. Each school can enter three per division.

**[P2]** You're teaching to grades four through six.

**[P1]** Junior High teaches six to eight minutes. Senior High, eight to ten.

**[P2]** You'll get a one-minute warning sign. When time's up, you're stopped. So practice with a timer.

### Your lesson

**[P1]** Every lesson needs an original audio or visual aid. Make it yourself.

**[P2]** The lesson has to be your own work, too. Borrow ideas and use reference books for background. Just don't copy someone else's lesson.

**[P1]** Keep class interaction to ten percent of your time or less. In an eight-minute lesson, that's under a minute.

### Your outline

*(Graphic: outline checklist.)*

**[P2]** When you present, hand the judges three typed copies of your lesson outline, plus three judging sheets.

**[P1]** No outline, and you're disqualified.

**[P2]** Your outline lists your objectives and the principles you'll teach.

**[P1]** Then your Scripture references, the materials you need, a short summary and a bibliography.

**[P2]** Remember Second Timothy two, verse two: "commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also." Teach like it matters, because it does.

### Outro

**[P1]** Teach to the kids. Bring a visual. Bring your outline.
