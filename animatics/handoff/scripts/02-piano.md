## Video 2: Music, Piano

**Run time:** about 2:30 · **For:** Sacred Piano, Classical and Piano Duet

*(Cold open: slow push-in on hands over the keys. Cut to presenters.)*

**[P1]** Two to five minutes. One piano. Three judges watching your hands. Let's get you ready.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for pianists.

### The categories

*(Graphic: three category cards.)*

**[P1]** There are three piano categories. Sacred Piano. Classical. And new this year, Piano Duet.

**[P2]** Each school can enter three students per division in Sacred, and three in Classical.

**[P1]** For duets, it's one per school in Junior High, and one in Senior High.

### Choosing your piece

**[P2]** Your piece has to be sacred or classical.

**[P1]** And "classical" means the style, not just the Classical era. Popular music doesn't count.

**[P2]** Movie soundtracks are out too, even the ones that sound classical. Same with Gospel rock, Gospel country, jazz and ragtime.

**[P1]** Play one piece or one medley, two to five minutes. Too short or too long costs a point per judge.

**[P2]** Elementary players, you won't lose points for running short.

**[P1]** Here's a big one. Is your classical piece from after 1950? Send it to the head piano judge for approval, at least a month before the competition.

**[P2]** And don't repeat the piece you played last year.

### The paperwork

*(B-roll: three copies and three judging sheets fanned out on a table.)*

**[P1]** When you're called, give the judges three copies of your music and three judging sheets.

**[P2]** Published music only. Clear copies, nothing handwritten.

**[P1]** Number every measure. Mark any changes you made to the printed music.

**[P2]** Each one you miss is a point per judge.

### On the day

**[P1]** Be there at the start of your performance hour, ready to play when they call you.

**[P2]** Introduce yourself, your school, your piece and the composer. Then the timing starts.

**[P1]** Soloists, memorize your piece. If it isn't memorized, it won't be scored.

**[P2]** Duet teams, you don't have to memorize.

*(Production note: confirm the duet rule before filming. See Booklet issues, item 1.)*

**[P1]** After you play, collect your copies. Anything left behind gets tossed.

### Outro

**[P2]** Pick the right piece.

**[P1]** Number every measure.

**[P2]** And memorize it.

**[P1]** Questions? Email us. The address is on your screen.

**[P2]** See you February twenty-fourth through the twenty-sixth at Pacific Baptist School!
