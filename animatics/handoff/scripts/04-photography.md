## Video 4: Photography

**Run time:** about 2:25 · **For:** Junior and Senior High photographers

*(Cold open: a phone lowers out of frame and a real camera comes up. Shutter click.)*

**[P1]** Put the phone down. Seriously. Phone photos are disqualified in SoCal Classic photography.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for photographers.

### The categories

*(Graphic: four category tiles with sample shots.)*

**[P1]** There are four categories. Human Portrait, Landscape, Still Life and Action.

**[P2]** Photography is for Junior and Senior High. Each school can enter two students per category.

**[P1]** You can enter one photo per category, in up to two categories.

**[P2]** And a photo you entered in a past year can't come back.

### The camera rules

**[P1]** Shoot on a real camera. A DSLR, a mirrorless or a point-and-shoot.

**[P2]** You can edit color, cropping, contrast and size. That's it.

**[P1]** You can't change what's in the picture. Nothing added, nothing removed, no distortion.

**[P2]** And no AI tools that change content. So no generative fill, and no sky replacement.

**[P1]** Do every edit yourself, and write each one down.

**[P2]** List the program you used. List every color change, and by what percent.

**[P1]** Then write down what you cropped and why, and how much you lightened or darkened it.

### The print

**[P2]** Print it at eight by ten. Any other size is disqualified. Color or black and white are both fine.

**[P1]** Slide it into a plastic sleeve. No frames.

### Check-in checklist

*(Graphic: checklist builds.)*

**[P2]** At check-in, bring your print in its sleeve and three judging sheets.

**[P1]** Staple a three-by-five card to the back of the sleeve. Name, school, grade and category.

**[P2]** Add an Artist's Idea Statement with each judging sheet. And your edit list, if you changed anything.

**[P1]** Last, bring a USB with your original RAW file.

**[P2]** Test that USB on another computer first. If it doesn't work, the judges can't check your photo, and it's disqualified.

### Outro

**[P1]** Real camera. Honest edits.

**[P2]** Eight by ten. Working USB.
