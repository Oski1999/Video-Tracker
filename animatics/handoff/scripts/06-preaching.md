## Video 6: Bible Preaching

**Run time:** about 2:15 · **For:** young men in Junior and Senior High

*(Cold open: a young man at a pulpit, Bible open, wide shot from the back of the room.)*

**[P1]** "Preach the word; be instant in season, out of season." Second Timothy four, verse two. Here's how preaching works at SoCal Classic.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for preachers.

### Who and what

*(Graphic: three sermon types.)*

**[P1]** Bible Preaching is for young men in Junior and Senior High. Each school can enter three per division.

**[P2]** Every preacher enters one category. There are three.

**[P1]** Expository. You preach a passage. Your points come straight from it, built around one main idea.

**[P2]** Evangelistic. It's aimed at the lost. The goal is to draw them to Christ.

**[P1]** And Topical. Your main points come from a topic, not from one text.

### Time

*(Graphic: countdown cards 3, 2, 1.)*

**[P2]** Junior High preaches six to eight minutes. Senior High, eight to ten.

**[P1]** Watch the back of the room. You'll see a sign at three minutes left, then two, then one.

**[P2]** Plan to land your conclusion inside your window.

### Your outline

**[P1]** When you get up to preach, hand the judges three typed copies of your outline, plus three judging sheets.

**[P2]** Your outline needs a title, a theme and your Scripture references.

**[P1]** Use standard outline form, in full sentences. Include your transitions, and write out your introduction and conclusion.

### Judging

**[P2]** Your judges are experienced preachers and Bible teachers. They use a preaching critique.

**[P1]** And every young man gets a written evaluation after his sermon.

**[P2]** Read it. It's one of the best parts of the day.

**[P1]** Other contestants and visitors are welcome to come listen. So bring your friends.

### Outro

**[P2]** Pick your type. Write your outline. Watch the signs.

**[P1]** Questions? Email us. The address is on your screen.

**[P2]** See you February twenty-fourth through the twenty-sixth at Pacific Baptist School!
