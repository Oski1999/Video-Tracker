## Video 3: Art

**Run time:** about 2:45 · **For:** Drawing, Watercolor, Oil or Acrylic, Monochromatic Drawing

*(Cold open: a framed piece hung on the judging wall, judge leaning in. Cut to presenters.)*

**[P1]** Your art gets judged before you say a word. So let's make sure it doesn't lose points before anyone looks.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for artists.

### The categories

*(Graphic: four categories with an example image each.)*

**[P1]** There are four. Drawing, Watercolor, Oil or Acrylic, and Monochromatic Drawing.

**[P2]** Monochromatic is Junior and Senior High only.

**[P1]** Each school can enter three students per category. And you can enter up to two pieces.

**[P2]** Drawing means charcoal, pencil or ink. Elementary can use one color or lots of colors.

**[P1]** Monochromatic means one color only. Think black on white, or white on black. Ink, pencil or charcoal.

**[P2]** Watercolor goes on real watercolor paper. Watercolor pencils are fine.

**[P1]** But you have to wash water over the whole piece. If you don't, it counts as a drawing, and it's disqualified.

**[P2]** And in watercolor, no tempera, no airbrush and no ink.

**[P1]** Oil or acrylic goes on canvas, canvas board, bristol board or heavy paper. No airbrush there either.

### The big rules

**[P2]** It has to be your own original work, made since last year's competition.

**[P1]** Getting advice is fine. Copying another artwork or photo in the same medium is not.

**[P2]** It has to be flat. No layers, no cut paper.

**[P1]** And it has to be totally dry. Wet art is disqualified.

**[P2]** Your subject doesn't have to be sacred. But no movie stars, no animation, and nothing mystical, pagan or worldly.

### Your checklist

*(Graphic: checklist builds one line at a time.)*

**[P1]** Mat it or frame it, or you lose three points. Keep the frame simple.

**[P2]** A framed piece needs a hanger on the back, ready to hang.

**[P1]** On the back, add a three-by-five card, face down. Put your full name, school, grade and category on it.

**[P2]** Then three judging sheets, stapled, in a plastic sleeve on the back. No judging sheets, and you're disqualified.

**[P1]** Last, attach an Artist's Idea Statement to each judging sheet. Tell us where your idea came from and how you developed it.
