## Video 5: Speech

**Run time:** about 2:50 · **For:** Poetry, Religious Reading, Humorous or Dramatic Interpretation

*(Cold open: a judge looks up and says "You may begin." Freeze frame.)*

**[P1]** In speech, the clock starts the second the judge says, "You may begin." Let's get you ready for that moment.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for speech.

### The categories

*(Graphic: three category cards.)*

**[P1]** Poetry is any poem. Funny, religious or dramatic. If it's a poem, it goes here, and only here.

**[P2]** Religious Reading is a persuasive or inspiring piece that someone else wrote. Like a speech, an essay or an editorial.

**[P1]** It needs a spiritual application that mentions God or Christ.

**[P2]** Then there's Humorous or Dramatic Interpretation. You take a scene from a play or a story and bring it to life.

**[P1]** Humorous is funny. Dramatic is serious. And if it has a spiritual application, it goes in Religious Reading instead.

**[P2]** Each school can enter three Elementary, two Junior High and three Senior High students per category.

**[P1]** And whatever you pick, memorize it.

*(Production note: see Booklet issues, item 5.)*

### Time limits

*(Graphic: three timers.)*

**[P2]** Elementary, you get a minute and a half to five minutes.

**[P1]** Junior High, three to eight minutes. Senior High, four to ten.

**[P2]** Come in under the minimum, and you lose ten points.

**[P1]** Thirty seconds before your time's up, a judge holds up a sign.

### On the day

**[P2]** Before you start, hand the judges three typed copies of your piece and three judging sheets.

**[P1]** Proofread those copies. The typing has to be correct.

**[P2]** The judge checks your name, your piece and the author. Then you hear, "You may begin." That's when timing starts.

**[P1]** Start with the title and the author. Don't know who wrote it? Just say, "Author unknown."

**[P2]** You can add a short intro to set the mood, and quick transitions. But all of that together is one minute, max. And it counts toward your time.

**[P1]** You get one prop. No costumes, no script, no lights, no sound effects and no stage makeup.

**[P2]** Keep it in good taste and doctrinally sound.

**[P1]** And pick something new. Not last year's piece, and not one someone else from your school is doing this year.

### Outro

**[P2]** Pick it right. Memorize it. Watch the clock.
