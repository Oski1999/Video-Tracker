## Video 9: Bible Memory

**Run time:** about 2:10 · **For:** memory contestants, all divisions

*(Cold open: a line of students facing the Bible Memory Master. "Time!")*

**[P1]** "Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee." Psalm one nineteen, verse eleven. Bible Memory is where you prove it.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for memorizers.

### The basics

*(Graphic: 2027 theme card, "The Triune God.")*

**[P1]** The 2027 theme is The Triune God. You'll be given the memory material, so get it early and start now.

**[P2]** Each school can enter two students per grade in Elementary. Junior and Senior High get four each. King James Version only.

**[P1]** It runs like a spelling bee. You stand in one line facing the Bible Memory Master, who goes left to right.

### The three rounds

*(Graphic: Round 1, 2, 3 cards.)*

**[P2]** Round one. The Master gives a reference. You quote the verse.

**[P1]** Round two. The Master quotes the verse. You give the reference.

**[P2]** Round three. The Master gives an application. You give the reference and the verse.

### The clock and the rules

**[P1]** The clock starts when the Master says, "Time!" You get ten seconds for a reference, and thirty-five for a verse.

**[P2]** Wait until the question's finished. Need to hear it again? Say, "Please repeat the question." You get that once per question.

**[P1]** A wrong reference is an error. So is any change to the verse. A word added, left out or swapped.

**[P2]** Mispronounce a word? That's not an error.

**[P1]** You can stop and start over. But you have to repeat exactly what you started with.

**[P2]** Two errors, and you're out. Last one standing wins.

### Outro

**[P1]** Learn it word for word. Know your references. Listen for "Time!"
