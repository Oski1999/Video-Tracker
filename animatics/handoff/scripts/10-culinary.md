## Video 10: Culinary Art

**Run time:** about 2:20 · **For:** Cookies and the Featured Dessert Challenge

*(Cold open: overhead shot of a banana loaf coming out of the oven.)*

**[P1]** This year, banana bread is a competition. Let's talk culinary.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for bakers.

### The categories

*(Graphic: Cookies card and Featured Dessert Challenge card.)*

**[P1]** There are two. Cookies, and the Featured Dessert Challenge.

**[P2]** For 2027, the challenge is Sweet Quick Breads.

**[P1]** Each school can enter three students per division in each one.

### Cookies

**[P2]** Bring one batch. Eight to twelve cookies, all from the same recipe.

**[P1]** Write the type of cookie on your recipe and your judging sheet.

**[P2]** Individual cookies only. No bar cookies. And yes, brownies count as bar cookies.

### Sweet Quick Breads

**[P1]** Quick breads are made without yeast. Think banana bread, zucchini bread, pumpkin bread or muffins.

**[P2]** Bring one standard loaf, whole and uncut. Or six standard muffins.

**[P1]** Glazes and add-ins are fine, as long as it's still a quick bread. No cupcakes, no cakes and no yeast breads.

### Rules for every entry

*(Graphic: checklist builds. Show "8½ x 11" on screen.)*

**[P2]** Make everything from scratch, by yourself. No boxed mixes and no premade dough.

**[P1]** Type your recipe on regular letter-size paper.

**[P2]** In the top right corner, put the recipe name, your name, school, grade and category. Then list every ingredient and every step.

**[P1]** Bring three copies of the recipe and three judging sheets. Attach a recipe to each sheet.

**[P2]** Serve it neatly on a disposable plate. And check your expiration dates.

**[P1]** Then get it to the right judging room before the deadline.

**[P2]** Judges score flavor, baking, appearance and creativity.

**[P1]** So what gets you disqualified? Fewer than three copies. Showing up late, or in the wrong room. The wrong item or count. Or anything not made from scratch.

### Outro
