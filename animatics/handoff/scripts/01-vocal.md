## Video 1: Music, Vocal Ensembles & Choirs

**Run time:** about 2:40 · **For:** small ensembles, large ensembles and choirs

*(Cold open: B-roll of a choir landing its last chord. Cut to both presenters.)*

**[P1]** You get three to five minutes on that stage. Let's make every second count.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for singers.

### The groups

*(Graphic: group sizes and entries per division.)*

**[P1]** First, how big is your group? Two to six singers is a small ensemble. Seven to twelve is a large ensemble. Thirteen or more is a choir.

**[P2]** Large ensembles and choirs compete together, in the same category.

**[P1]** Each school can bring three small ensembles and two large groups per division. That's Elementary, Junior High and Senior High.

**[P2]** Your accompanist doesn't count. Not toward group size, and not toward grade level.

### What to sing

*(Graphic: green checks and red X's.)*

**[P1]** Pick one song or one medley, three to five minutes long. Go over or under, and you lose a point per judge.

**[P2]** Unless you're Elementary. You won't lose points for running short.

**[P1]** Every song is accompanied live, on piano. No tracks.

**[P2]** And skip the Gospel rock, Gospel country, jazz and ragtime.

**[P1]** Doing a medley? Write every transition and bridge into the music.

**[P2]** Oh, and pick something new. You can't repeat last year's song.

### The paperwork

*(B-roll: hands numbering measures with a pencil.)*

**[P2]** When they call your group, hand the judges three copies of your music and three judging sheets.

**[P1]** Use published music. Clear copies, nothing handwritten.

**[P2]** Number every measure. Then mark any changes, like a key change or a cut.

**[P1]** Miss either one, and it's a point per judge.

### On the day

**[P1]** Show up at the start of your performance hour. Be ready when they call you, even if things run early.

**[P2]** Step up and introduce your group, your school, your song and the composer. Then the clock starts.

**[P1]** Everything is memorized. If it isn't, it won't be scored.

**[P2]** A director can lead you. But no choreography and no big movement. Let the music do the work.

**[P1]** When you're done, grab your copies back from the judges. Anything left behind gets tossed.

### Outro

**[P2]** So, memorize it.

**[P1]** Number it.

**[P2]** And time it.
