## Video 8: Bible Quizzing

**Run time:** about 2:45 · **For:** quiz teams and coaches, all divisions

*(Cold open: quiz lights flash on, quizmaster points. "Number three!")*

**[P1]** Twenty points. Thirty seconds. First light on gets the shot. Bible Quizzing is the fastest event at SoCal Classic.

**[P2]** Welcome to the SoCal Classic Fine Arts explainers. This one's for quizzers.

### This year's material

*(Graphic: 2027 books.)*

**[P1]** For 2027, Junior and Senior High study Mark, plus First, Second and Third John, and Jude.

**[P2]** Elementary studies Mark. King James Version only.

### Your team

**[P1]** Each school brings one team per division. That's two to five quizzers, plus two subs.

**[P2]** Elementary is fourth through sixth grade. Junior High is seventh and eighth. Senior High is ninth through twelfth.

**[P1]** One official coach sits with the team. And if someone's missing when you're called, you still quiz.

### How a game works

*(Graphic: simple scoreboard.)*

**[P2]** Early rounds can have three teams in one match.

**[P1]** The Quizmaster reads a question. The first quizzer to light up answers. You get ten seconds to start, and thirty to finish.

**[P2]** A right answer is twenty points. Senior High games run to fifteen correct answers. Elementary and Junior High run to ten.

**[P1]** Miss it, and the question goes to the other teams as a toss-up.

**[P2]** Miss that, and there's a free question worth ten, with no penalty.

**[P1]** Get four right, or five in Senior High, and you quiz out. A sub comes in.

**[P2]** Make four errors, or five in Senior High, and you're out too.

**[P1]** Your team's first three errors are free. After that, every error costs ten points.

### Answering right

**[P2]** On finish-the-verse questions, it's word for word. One word added, missing or swapped is an error.

**[P1]** On reference questions, you can put it in your own words. Quoting the verse word for word there is an error.

**[P2]** Captains get three challenges a game, or four in Senior High.

**[P1]** Stand up and say, "Excuse me, Mr. Quizmaster. I would like to challenge."

**[P2]** It's double elimination. Every team has to lose twice to go home.

**[P1]** And no video or recording during competition.

### Outro

**[P2]** "Study to shew thyself approved unto God." Start studying Mark now.
